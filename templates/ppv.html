About Queue

What is the enrichment queue?

The enrichment queue manages PPV channels waiting to be matched with event data from TheSportsDB. Channels are automatically queued during account syncs, but you can manually queue or reprocess them here.

Queue States:

  • Queued: Waiting to be processed
  • Processing: Currently being matched
  • Matched: Successfully enriched
  • No Match: Event not found in database
  • Error: Processing failed
